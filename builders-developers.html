What we handle

  • Lot and acreage clearing: brush, undergrowth and small trees, taken down to workable ground.
  • Rough grading and pad prep so the next trade can get on site.
  • Hauling of cleared material, spoil and job-site debris, so you deal with one crew from clearing to cleanup.
  • Infill, spec and multi-lot programs across Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco and Manatee counties.

Why repeat customers stay with one crew

  • One point of contact who answers the phone and knows your lots.
  • A written price after the site visit, so there are no surprises when the invoice arrives.
  • Before and after photos on each lot for your file.
  • We schedule around your trades and tell you when a start date moves.

How to get a price

  1. Send the address (or lot number and subdivision) and what you need done.
  2. We set up a site visit and confirm scope and access.
  3. You get a written price and a target start date.

Have several lots or a recurring need? Say so when you call and we will talk about pricing for repeat work. Need a W-9 or a certificate of insurance for your vendor file? Ask and we will send them.

Permit and tree-protection rules vary by city and county and can be stricter near wetlands or for protected trees. Bring them up early and we will go over them with you before the work is scheduled.
