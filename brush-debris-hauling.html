Hauling & Debris Removal · Tampa Bay

Brush and debris hauling in Tampa Bay

Clearing a lot leaves a lot of material. We haul it off with our own F-350 and dump trailer, so there is one crew to call from the first cut to the last load.

Call (813) 683-5127 Email Us

What we haul

  • Cleared brush, vegetation and small trees
  • Spoil and excess dirt from grading
  • Fill dirt
  • Job-site debris

Hauling is part of the clearing and grading jobs we do. If you need hauling on its own, call and describe the load and we will tell you whether it is a fit.

Our hauling equipment

EquipmentUse
2018 Ford F‑350 6.2L duallyHeavy-duty truck for equipment transport and towing
16×7×5 ft dump trailerDebris, spoil and material removal. The box holds roughly 20 cubic yards by volume; dirt and spoil are heavy, so weight rather than space sets the load.

Why haul-off with the clearing crew matters

When clearing and hauling are one job, there is one schedule, one point of contact, and no pile of debris left sitting on your lot while you wait for a separate hauler.

What to tell us

  • What the material is: brush, dirt, mixed debris
  • Roughly how much, and where it sits on the property
  • How trucks and trailers get in: gate width, soft ground, overhead lines
  • Photos of the pile or area, if you can send them

What affects the price

  • The volume of material and the number of loads
  • The type of material: brush is light and bulky, dirt is heavy
  • The distance to disposal
  • Access to the pile
