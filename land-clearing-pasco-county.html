Land clearing in Pasco County

Pasco County has plenty of open lots and acreage headed for homes, barns and outbuildings, and just as many overgrown parcels that owners want cleaned up. We clear brush and small trees, grade the cleared area if you need it, and haul the debris off.

What we do here

  • Land clearing: brush, small trees and undergrowth cleared from residential lots and small acreage
  • Grading and site prep: building pads, driveway approaches and drainage areas, to the plan you provide
  • Hauling: cleared material and debris taken off with our own truck and dump trailer

Where we work in Pasco County

Land O’ Lakes, Wesley Chapel, Zephyrhills, Dade City, New Port Richey, Port Richey, Hudson, Holiday, Trinity, San Antonio and the surrounding communities.

Permits and rules in Pasco County

  • Unincorporated Pasco County. The county requires a permit for tree removal in unincorporated areas, with exemptions. Its residential tree removal requirements include limits for parcels that do not yet have a primary structure, so it is worth calling the county before clearing a vacant lot.
  • What the county asks for. Applications include a site plan or survey showing where the trees sit relative to the parcel, and photos. The county lists an average review time of three to four business days for complete applications.
  • Cities inside Pasco. Incorporated cities such as Zephyrhills, Dade City and New Port Richey set their own rules. Contact the city if your property is inside its limits.

Rules and fees change, so confirm with the county or your city before work starts. Details on this page were checked on the official pages on September 18, 2026.
